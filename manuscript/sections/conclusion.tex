\section{Conclusion}
APBR-SAM carries information from dense optimization into structured support selection. Global energy feedback learns persistent coordinate scales under a fixed perturbation budget. At pruning time, those scales determine the first deletion in each quartet; the second decision uses the reconstruction state induced by a virtual OBS update. Joint compensation then reconstructs the layer for the selected support. This division of roles gives the training geometry a direct entry point into pruning while using activation correlations to complete and compensate the decision. The resulting method connects historical perturbation allocation, conditional support selection, and second-order reconstruction in a single post-training 2:4 pipeline.
