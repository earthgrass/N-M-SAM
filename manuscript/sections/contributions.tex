Our contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
\item A persistent coordinate-geometry mechanism that reallocates perturbation energy under a fixed global $L_2$ radius and carries the resulting training state into post-training support selection.
\item A geometry-first conditional pruning procedure: APBR selects the initial deletion, the next decision uses its predicted reconstruction effect, and one joint physical OBS update compensates the selected pair.
\item A controlled one-shot 2:4 evaluation protocol that isolates the initial geometric decision through matched dense parents, calibration inputs, and reconstruction settings.
\end{itemize}
